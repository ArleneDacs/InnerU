Welcome to InnerU support. If you need help with your account, wellness tracking features, coach chat, notifications, or anything else in the app, contact our team and we will do our best to assist you.

Support email: support@valenin.com

How We Can Help

  • Login or sign-in link issues
  • Profile and account questions
  • Step tracking, fasting, sleep, meditation, and calorie tracker issues
  • Coach messaging and community feature concerns
  • Bug reports and app performance problems
  • Privacy, data, or account deletion requests

When You Contact Us

Please include the email address associated with your InnerU account, your device model, your operating system version, and a short description of the issue so we can help you faster.
