Privacy Policy for InnerU

Effective date: May 15, 2026 · Last updated: July 27, 2026

InnerU, operated by Valenin, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the InnerU mobile app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, including photos or wellness preferences.
  • Health and wellness activity data you enter or generate in the app, such as steps, sleep, fasting, meditation, and calorie tracking information.
  • Device and technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic data.
  • Location data if you grant permission for features that depend on location access.
  • Camera access if you grant permission for features that require scanning or image capture.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide wellness tracking, coaching, reminders, and app features.
  • To improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To communicate service-related notices, updates, and support responses.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and enforce our terms.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app, including authentication, database, storage, analytics, and hosting providers. We may also share information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your account, profile, and health and wellness activity data for as long as your account remains active, so that we can provide the app's tracking, coaching, and history features. We do not keep this data longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

  • If you delete your account, or request that we delete it, we permanently delete your account record and associated activity data (such as profile details, journal and mood entries, exercise, fasting, calorie, sleep, and goal data) from our production systems right away as part of that request.
  • Copies of data may briefly persist in encrypted backups after deletion until those backups are rotated out in the ordinary course, after which they are purged.
  • Device, diagnostic, and crash data collected through third-party crash reporting is retained by that provider under its own standard retention window before it is deleted.
  • We may retain limited information longer than the periods above where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or prevent fraud and abuse, and we delete or anonymize it once those purposes no longer apply.

Your Choices

  • You may update certain profile information inside the app.
  • You may disable permissions such as location, camera, or notifications in your device settings.
  • You may contact us to request account or data deletion, subject to legal and operational requirements, as described above under Data Retention.

Children's Privacy

InnerU is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Valenin at support@valenin.com.
